# Synthetic sample assets

`sample-landscape.png` and `sample-dusk.png` are mathematical RGB gradients, not
photographs. To the extent possible, their contributors dedicate them to the
public domain under CC0-1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
.

No private image, upstream photo collection, downloaded artwork or person
appears in either asset. Their sizes differ deliberately to exercise image
decoding. Your own local images are not covered by this dedication; keep them
outside the public package and confirm their separate permissions.
